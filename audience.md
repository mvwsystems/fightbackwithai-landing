# audience.md — who this is for

## The avatar
Self-employed and billing for their time. Coaches, consultants, freelancers, contractors, creators. Usually one person, sometimes one person plus a VA. Revenue is real; hours are the constraint. They do every job themselves and most of the admin happens after 9pm.

They have tried ChatGPT. It gave them garbage. They concluded it was the tool.

## What they want, in order
1. A job gone. Not understood, not optimized. Gone by Friday.
2. Math they can check. Hours back, dollars saved, at their own rate.
3. Respect. One operator talking to another. No hype, no hand-holding.

## Not for
People who want to learn about AI, collect tools, or read think pieces. Anyone looking for an income promise.

## Product constraints that follow
- Every issue names one job, one skill, one number, one move.
- Every move is finishable in 90 minutes on a real piece of their business.
- Every number is conservative and checkable. Dollar figures describe the model taught, never a promised outcome.
- Reading time under five minutes. Plain steps. No jargon without a one-line explanation.
- Page works on a phone at 375px; that's where most of them read at night.
- Proof is the ledger and the math. No testimonials, logos, badges, or timers.

## What makes them leave
Volume. Adjectives. Anything that sounds like a LinkedIn ghostwriter. Being sold to before being handed something.
