# soul.md — why Fight Back with AI exists

One person is running a business that used to take ten people. Proposals, books, listings, support, follow-up, invoicing, content, research, scheduling, onboarding. The work gets done after 9pm and the margin goes to whoever they hire to do the parts they can't.

AI can take most of those jobs. Not the business, not the judgment, the jobs. Nobody is showing solopreneurs how, in plain steps, on their own work, without selling them a course first.

Fight Back with AI is the record of one person doing exactly that.

## The mission line (verbatim, everywhere)
“I build AI businesses with my own time and money and show you everything — so we both get free.”

## The one promise
One skill a week that removes a job from your plate. What it's worth in hours or dollars. One thing to ship tonight. If an issue can't deliver all three, it doesn't go out.

## The enemy
The ten jobs. Not AI, not the reader, not other people. The math of one person doing all of it.

## What we are not
Not a guru. Not a course funnel. Not AI commentary. Not a tool roundup. Nothing is sold to overwhelm; the newsletter is free because Matt is building the businesses anyway and the newsletter is the record of what held up.

## The test for every decision
Does this hand a solopreneur a job they can be rid of by Friday? If not, cut it.
