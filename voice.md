# voice.md — how Fight Back with AI writes

Structure from Dan Kennedy. Voice from Justin Welsh.

## Structure (Kennedy)
- Specificity over adjectives. A number beats a claim.
- ROI math the reader can check, with conservative inputs stated.
- Disqualify. Say who it's not for.
- A reason-why for everything free.
- Proof stacked in a ledger, not testimonials.
- A promise with consequences, not a disclaimer.

## Voice (Welsh)
- One operator talking to another. Terse. Calm.
- Short declaratives. Fragments are fine.
- Numbers without hype.
- No exclamation points.
- Restraint reads as competence. Volume reads as a pitch.

## Banned
unlock, supercharge, game-changer, 10x, leverage, AI-powered, cutting-edge, “in today's fast-paced world”, any income promise, any emoji.

## Calibration lines
Write next to these. If a sentence would embarrass them, cut it.
- “If we can't put a number on it, we don't send it.”
- “Done is better than perfect.”
- “Run your last real proposal through the stack tonight. Compare.”
- “Count how many of these you did after 9pm this week.”

## Patterns on the page
- Eyebrow: two to four words, tracked caps. THE MATH. THE LEDGER. FROM MATT.
- Headline: Title Case, short, one accent phrase. “For Operators. Not Spectators.”
- Body: no section over ~120 words. The page gets longer by adding blocks, never by fattening one.
- Sign-off: “— Matt”, in the accent color.
